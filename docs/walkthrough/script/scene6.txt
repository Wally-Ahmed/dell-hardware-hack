And here is the heart of it: consent. Every person who appears in the footage is detected and given a status. Approved, unknown, or remove. Unknowns are never silently passed. Generated output is re-checked, so an unapproved face cannot ship. And if you ask for a shot with someone unapproved, the request is refused before the expensive generation even starts. Every generated file carries its receipt: what made it, from what, approved by whom. Policy-enforced. Audited. Human-signed-off.
