Here is how a film actually gets finished. A shot doesn't work, and fixing it means a reshoot: crew, location, cast, booked all over again. That costs real money, and usually the schedule will not allow it. And the footage itself is the most legally sensitive asset a production owns. It is unreleased, and it is full of real people's faces. Now, generative AI can already fix shots no reshoot budget could afford. But all of those tools live in the cloud, and a studio cannot send unreleased footage to the cloud. So the capability exists. The people who need it most cannot touch it.
